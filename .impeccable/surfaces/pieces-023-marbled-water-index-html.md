# Marbled Water

Mode: Experience. A portable, dependency-free browser artwork.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Floating pigment occupies one shared moving surface. Each drop inserts
color, displaces earlier pigment, and joins the current. User explicitly approved
this correction to the prior disconnected background, stripe overlay, and blobs.

OWN-WORLD: The supplied photograph pins mineral blues, pale cool water, dense
fine folds, and restrained teal, ochre, and plum. The existing compact draggable
control panel remains subordinate to the artwork.

STORY: Watch a color spread and follow the changes it causes in neighboring
veins. Click or tap to add pigment. Space pauses; R starts a new bath.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Edge-to-edge blue marbling at several scales, with sparse mineral
seams embedded in the same field. No separate lower band. A small control panel
at upper left shows the cache version. The entire bath is the interaction area.

FORM: User-pinned Suminagashi reference and explicitly authorized implementation;
no concept selection or seed roll required. GPU pigment transport with continuous
currents and incremental area displacement is the signature interaction.

FINISH: Watch the running result for at least 10 seconds, inspect an evolved
frame, desktop and narrow screen, and check interactions. Report an honest
aesthetic judgment separately from functional verification. No photographic
realism claim. Keep the renderer and its fallback self-contained.
