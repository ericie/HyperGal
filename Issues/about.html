HypermediaArcade

Hypermedia Arcade begins with influential games and asks “What if?” We take games whose rules and images have become part of a shared vocabulary, then change something fundamental: the objective, the opponent, the reward, or the need for a player.

Those changes are a way of thinking through play. What if success made the problem bigger? What if defending yourself kept the conflict alive? A familiar game becomes a satire when its rules start to reveal something about the systems we live with: how we compete, accumulate, survive, and define progress.

These works are made to be played and watched. You can enter a game and act within its rules, or step back and let the system perform. Each mode offers a different way to understand it: what it feels like to participate, and what becomes visible when it carries on without you.

Hypermedia Club extends my earlier work making interactive art and arcade projects. This project is a solo venture: I work with AI agents as thought and coding partners to develop ideas, write code, and work through revisions.
