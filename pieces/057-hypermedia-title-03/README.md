# Hypermedia Title 03 — Connect the Dots

Custom single-stroke cursive spelling **Hypermedia Club** on one line. A Logo-style turtle connects evenly spaced dots, following the pen strokes through each letter from left to right. The pen lifts between words and to dot the i. The drawing holds briefly before repeating.

Click Pause or press Space to pause/resume. Click Replay or press R to start over. Reduced-motion preferences show the static dotted title until Play is selected.

Self-contained SVG and vanilla JavaScript; no fonts, libraries, or network dependencies. SVG scales the same one-line composition to every viewport.
