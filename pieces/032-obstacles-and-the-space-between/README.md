# Obstacles, and The Space Between

A dry-garden companion to **Obstacles and How to Avoid Them** and
**Obstacles and How to Go Around Them**.

The current study is intentionally reduced to one stone and one samon system.
The stone inherits the plan-view language of the petals study: an irregular
survey-like footprint, quiet gray-beige fill, dark outline, two nested contours,
sparse flecks, and a small specimen number. Nested closed furrows follow the
stone's irregular footprint and expand through the gravel without crossing or
passing beneath the stone.

The floating panel currently exposes only the interval between rake furrows.
The other garden principles and additional stones have been removed until this
base case is resolved.

This is a construction stage, not the final garden composition.

Click or tap the gravel, or press `R`, Space, or Enter, to generate a new garden.
The panel can be dragged by its header or collapsed. Add `?seed=<value>` to the
URL for a repeatable starting composition.

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser. The artwork has no runtime
dependencies.
