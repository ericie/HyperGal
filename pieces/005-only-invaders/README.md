# Only Invaders

A Space Invaders riff with the invaders removed.

Four defender cannons ride fixed color-matched worlds at the top, right, bottom,
and left edges of a clean star field. Each landmass is the shallow visible cap
of a giant circle centered far beyond the screen. Every cannon has one degree
of travel along its own arc and tilts to stay planted on the changing curve.
Each cannon is rendered entirely in its own color, without targeting guides.
Every projectile inherits the exact color of the cannon that fired it. The
cannons take turns sending bullets directly toward the opposite edge,
sometimes as single shots and sometimes as sudden aggressive volleys. North
only attacks south, east only attacks west, and vice versa. Occasional rapid
spasms burn through five to eight rounds in a fraction of a second.

Any armed cannon other than the attacker may volunteer to intercept an incoming
bullet. The threatened defender often answers, but either adjacent cannon can
cut across the field and erase it instead. Reaction times, intercept heights,
aim points, lateral lunges, volley cadences, and defensive urgency all vary.
Some saves are clean and early. Others wait until the last possible instant.

Every ordinary projectile fired consumes one of forty-three rounds from that
defender's stockpile. The remaining ammunition is shown as large white marks
along its edge—there is no numeric counter or score. Empty slots remain as faint
outlines.
There are no charged super bullets during ordinary play.

The final position in every ammunition rail is an oversized white circle instead
of a cartridge. A cannon holding this last round swells and flashes before firing it.
The oversized boss round takes on that cannon's color, pulses, and travels
directly toward the opposing cannon while the rest of the crossfire continues.
Only the threatened cannon breaks off to fire furiously at it; every other
platform races toward the rail endpoint farthest from the boss round.
Each defensive hit makes the boss round a little larger but can never destroy
or stop it. The round always reaches the opposing side, where contact detonates
a pulsing, screen-shaking white-hot shockwave that kills every cannon and wipes
the screen blank.
