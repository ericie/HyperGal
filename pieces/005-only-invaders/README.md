# Only Invaders

A Space Invaders riff with the invaders removed.

Four defender cannons hold the top, right, bottom, and left edges of the
screen. They take turns sending bullets across the empty field, sometimes as
single shots and sometimes as sudden aggressive volleys. Every attack now
travels all the way toward one named edge, and only the defender on that edge
may answer it with a countershot.

While a targeted side still has a round to answer with, the countershot never
misses—but the dancers are intentionally a little off-beat: reaction times,
intercept heights, aim points, lateral lunges, volley cadences, and defensive
urgency all vary. Some saves are clean and early. Others wait until the last
possible instant before erasing the incoming bullet.

Once in a while, a defender stops to charge and fires a larger super bullet. It
is still doomed to be caught, but when the countershot destroys it, the whole
screen flashes white and shakes under the blast. The blast clears every other
bullet from the field, but it does not restore any ammunition.

Every projectile fired consumes one of thirty rounds from that defender's
stockpile. The remaining ammunition is shown as a row of tiny cartridges and a
countdown on each corresponding edge. Empty slots remain as faint outlines.

No defender fires its final cartridge as an ordinary shot. When a cannon next
tries to fire with one round left, it deploys that round as a suicide nuke. The
cannon disappears at the point of detonation and a white shockwave expands
across the field, destroying every opposing cannon as it reaches them. Their
unused ammunition remains visible but inert after mutual destruction.
