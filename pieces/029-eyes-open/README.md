# Eyes Open

A field of unpaired eyes opening and closing independently. On a portrait phone,
roughly fifteen to eighteen are present at once; larger displays gain more eyes
without becoming a rigid grid.

## Drawing

Each eye is a pair of quadratic lid curves clipped around a flat, colored iris
and black pupil. The restricted palette and hard edges borrow the screen-printed
graphic language of the reference while the pure black field keeps the piece
spatial rather than face-like. Positions, sizes, slight rotations, colors, and
gaze offsets all change when an eye returns.

## Motion

An eye eases open, holds, then closes quickly. Once the two lids meet, nothing
remains on screen—not even a closed-eye line. After a short absence, that eye is
recycled at a new position. Lifecycles are staggered so the field is already
populated when the piece loads and never blinks in unison.

With reduced motion enabled, the animation resolves to a still field of open
eyes. Animation also pauses while the page is hidden.

## Archival rule

The folder is fully self-contained: one HTML file, one metadata file, and one
thumbnail. There are no dependencies, external assets, or build requirements.
