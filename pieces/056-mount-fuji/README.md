# Mount Fuji

A fixed oil landscape inspired by Van Gogh, made entirely from short, curved
brush strokes with the paint renderer from **Loaded Brush**. Blue and gold
marks curl around the sun; small hooks follow Fuji’s snowy slopes, and broken
crescents shimmer across the lake. Olive and ochre dabs form the foreground.
Every mark retains bristle grooves, raised impasto, linen and wet highlights.

Open `index.html` directly; no dependencies or build step. Requires WebGL 2
and floating-point render targets. The strokes are deliberately not editable.
The complete painting appears immediately, with no continuous animation.
**S** or **Save PNG** saves the canvas. `?seed=your-word` varies the bristles
and small details while keeping the composition. Portrait screens fit the
whole landscape with linen above and below.

The thumbnail is a 720 × 450 canvas capture of this sketch at a 1440 × 900
viewport with the default `fuji` seed; it contains no generated image asset.
