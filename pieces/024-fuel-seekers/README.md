# Fuel Seekers

A closed traffic system laid over a quiet resource map. The underlayer borrows
the domain-warped noise, folded currents, veins, halos, and mineral grain of
`Marbles`, but compresses them into a near-black oil field. Ten stations are
selected from the richest points in that computed field rather than scattered
at random.

Sixty translucent bubbles move continuously among the stations. The amber
level inside each bubble is its actual fuel. Motion consumes it by distance;
an unoccupied station stops one arriving bubble and fills it to the top. A
station is not reserved while a bubble travels, so several bubbles can converge
and late arrivals may have to divert. A bubble that reaches zero pops. Its
replacement starts just outside one of the four edges with a partial tank.

Press `R` to generate a new oil field, relocate the stations to its deposits,
and restart the population. Add `?seed=anything` to the URL to revisit a field.
With `prefers-reduced-motion` active, the system holds on a fully composed still
frame.

## Archival rule

This folder is fully self-contained. It uses browser-native HTML, CSS,
JavaScript, and Canvas 2D with no external assets or dependencies.
