# No Empty Places

No Empty Places is a deep indigo field crossed by chalk-white currents. The
painting grows through a Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion system: two virtual
materials spread at different rates, react when they meet, and continually
consume and replenish one another.

Small seeded deposits begin throughout the field. Their edges lengthen, collide,
turn back, and settle into a dense labyrinth. The pattern has no prescribed
turns or underlying grid, so broad bends, tight hairpins, enclosed pockets, and
quiet parallel runs arise at different scales in every iteration.

The simulation wraps at the boundaries before being enlarged with smooth
sampling, allowing the pattern to reach every edge without a visible frame.
Subtle pigment variation and softened transitions keep the two-color surface
closer to paint than to a binary diagram.

## Interaction

- **Click or tap** — grow a new painting from a new seed
- **Space or Enter** — grow a new painting from a new seed
- **R** — replay the current seed
- `?seed=` — revisit a particular painting

Reduced-motion preferences render the completed field immediately. The piece
uses vanilla HTML, CSS, and Canvas with no rendering dependencies.
