# Moon Orchard

An endless lunar patrol below; a living record of its fire above. Inspired by the jumping buggy, paired guns, rocks and flying saucers of Moon Patrol. Original canvas drawing, no borrowed assets or dependencies.

The buggy automatically jumps pits and approaching bombs and fires forward and upward. Upward shots that intersect the globe plant trees in its rotating coordinate system. Each tree retains its angle, shape, colour and planting time in localStorage, along with the accumulated rotation. Growth follows simulation time, so pausing freezes the entire scene. Reloading restores the forest; the world does not advance while closed. Storage failure leaves the scene running with a session-only forest.

Click or Space pauses. S downloads a PNG. Resize preserves the forest. Fully self-contained; open index.html directly. Browser storage belongs to the browser and origin, so moving the folder or clearing site data may give it a new forest.
