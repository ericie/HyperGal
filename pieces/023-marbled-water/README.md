# Marbled Water

A simulation of paper marbling on a floating bath. The default Mineral Current
mode begins with blue-gray washes and layered ink families, then carries every
contour through one bath-wide field of sweeping currents, small eddies, and
fine cross-ripples. New ink drops arrive one at a time in a quick, irregular
rhythm. Each grows over a slightly wider open-water halo, reserving a pale
interval around the ink while keeping its center saturated. Every bloom
displaces the older colors and open spaces together.

The bath opens already richly worked, with several families traveling along
curved paths rather than sitting as geometric targets. Indigo, slate, powder
blue, and cool gray carry most of the surface. Oxidized gold, deep teal, and
plum are deliberately smaller deposits, so they stretch into occasional fine
veins instead of taking over the bath. Drop diameter, gap width, and timing all
vary substantially. Exact-center runs are now rare and brief. Other palettes
remain continuous; Mineral Current alone preserves pale water between ink
bands. A light field of suspended pigment flecks breaks the digitally perfect
surface, while the visible bath border contains every displaced contour.

Once ink has covered nearly the entire bath and held there for several seconds,
one enormous pale drop expands beyond every edge. Its color becomes the next
bath ground, clearing the saturated field so a new cycle can accumulate on a
visibly different surface.

Click or tap the bath to place an ink drop manually. Press Space to pause or
resume, and R to start a new bath. Add `?seed=anything` to revisit a bath or
`&panel=0` for a clean capture.

## Archival rule

This folder is fully self-contained. It uses browser-native Canvas 2D and
JavaScript with no external assets or dependencies.
