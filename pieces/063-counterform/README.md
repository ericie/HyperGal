# Counterform

An animated two-color study inspired by a shop window: regular purple and cream
stripes meet soft, circulating contours. Eight bodies rise and sink on staggered
100–149 second cycles, wobbling and stretching as they move. They briefly suggest
hearts, cat heads and skulls, then melt back into blobs. Each body visits all
three silhouettes over a 138 second morph cycle, independent of its motion.

Each organic body reverses the stripe colors. Overlapping bodies reverse them
again. Four bodies have hollow centers; skull morphs dissolve the central hole
and open two eye sockets and a small nasal opening. The stripes stay still.

Click, Space, or Enter pauses and resumes. S saves the current frame as PNG.
Reduced motion starts paused. Animation sleeps while the document is hidden.
No visible titles, labels, or controls. The canvas carries an accessible
description and keyboard focus outline.

Open `index.html` directly. Native Canvas 2D only; no dependencies or network
requests. The thumbnail is a browser-rendered frame of the actual piece.
