# Hypermedia Title 02

A self-contained full-screen Plinko machine spelling `HYPERMEDIA`.
Ten large letter disks, each with its own color, fall at a controlled speed through a sparse
field of small white pegs on black. Every impact paints a peg with the disk's
color. The disks leave the bottom of the screen and recycle from the top forever.
They also collide with and rebound from one another, with every impact feeding
visible angular momentum into the letterforms.
New drops reserve and aim across the remaining white pegs in the top row. Once
that row is fully colored, later drops enter through the open lanes between pegs.
Every entry varies its horizontal position, orientation, spin, and slight
sideways drift.

On narrow screens, every peg row spans to within ten pixels of both edges. This
closes the side pockets created by a conventional staggered Plinko layout, so a
disk meeting the outer pegs is deflected back into the field instead of settling
against the wall.

Click anywhere or press Space to pause and resume. People who prefer reduced
motion receive a static excerpt.

## Archival rule

The piece uses a locally vendored Public Sans variable font and a small
requestAnimationFrame physics simulation, with no runtime network requests.
