# To Big for This World

An endless maze, one Pac, and power pellets.

Every walkable tile arrives with a pellet. Pac continuously chooses the nearest
remaining pellet by shortest maze path and eats it. As Pac approaches an edge,
the camera begins to follow and new connected maze chunks grow beyond the frame
in that direction. Chunk doorways match on every side, so the maze and its trail
of pellets can expand forever in any direction.

Pac begins small and grows with every pellet. Once its body is wider than a
corridor, the maze and nearby pellets bend outward to make room. Pac can keep
turning at any size and grows to several times the tunnel width. Time spent
crossing already-cleared corridors makes Pac starve and shrink, allowing the
maze to relax back into its original grid.

Distance traveled carries the walls through a continuous rainbow cycle:
blue, indigo, violet, red, orange, yellow, green, then blue again.

There are no ghosts, collisions, controls, captions, counters, or secondary
behaviors in this version.
