# Moss Eaters

A colony of small, segmented grazers eats its way through a damp moss field.
Each animal steers toward the richest nearby growth. Its mouth clears a narrow
central route while alternating bites expose short ribs on either side, so a
long feeding path begins to resemble a pale fern or a frost dendrite.

The surface opens with several generations of older trails already present.
Living grazers continue at the tips, occasionally separating into new paths.
Crossings, abandoned routes, and slow moss regrowth keep the image between a
biological process and an all-over drawing.

Click or tap to hatch a small clutch at that point. Press R or Enter to grow a
new field. Press Space to pause and resume. Reduced-motion preferences produce
a densely grazed still image; clicks add complete local feeding paths.

The piece is self-contained vanilla HTML, CSS, and Canvas with no runtime
dependencies. Add `?seed=1234` to the URL to revisit a particular field.
